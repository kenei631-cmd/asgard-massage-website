What is Swedish Massage?

Swedish Massage is a traditional massage approach. At Asgard, it is customized through a conversation about what you want from the session.

Can I request a certain pressure?

Yes. Share your preferences before and during the appointment so the session can be adjusted around your comfort.

Should I choose Swedish or Wellness Massage?

Choose Swedish when that familiar approach is what you are looking for. Choose Wellness when you want a flexible, broader customized session or are not sure which approach fits.
