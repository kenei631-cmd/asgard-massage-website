For the way you move

Start with your activity and your goal.

Sports massage is a useful name when you want to connect your appointment to an active routine, a specific activity, or the way your body feels before or after movement.

You do not need a perfect plan. Tell your therapist what you have been doing, what is on your calendar, and how you want the hour to feel. Your preferences are part of the conversation.
