A little reassurance

Questions are welcome.

A first visit is a good time to ask about pressure, session style, or anything else that would help you feel more comfortable.

What if I do not know what kind of massage to book?

That is completely okay. Book a massage, or call the studio to ask for a starting recommendation.

Can I tell the therapist what I prefer?

Yes. Your goals and comfort are important information for the session. Share your preferences before and during the appointment.

What if I have an injury-related question?

Call before booking so the team can explain the current process for qualifying medical-massage appointments.
