Focused, not formulaic

Start with a conversation, not a preset routine.

Deep tissue massage is a name for approaches that give more deliberate attention to deeper soft-tissue layers and the areas you name. It is not a fixed level of pressure or a one-size-fits-all session.

Before and during your appointment, let your therapist know what feels helpful, what feels like too much, and where you would like more or less attention. Your feedback is part of the service.

Questions, answered

Clearer expectations before you arrive.

The best session starts with an open conversation. If you are unsure which massage path fits, the studio can help you choose a simple next step.

Does Deep Tissue Massage have to hurt?

No. Firm pressure is not a substitute for communication. Let your therapist know how the pressure feels at any point so the session can be adjusted around your comfort.

Do I need to know exactly where I want the session to focus?

No. You can share what has been bothering you, what you would like more attention on, and what you hope the session feels like. That conversation helps guide the appointment.

Should I choose Deep Tissue or Wellness Massage?

Choose Deep Tissue when you are looking for more focused, firmer attention in particular areas. Choose Wellness Massage when you want a broader customized session or are not sure which approaches fit best.

What if I need injury-related billing or claim guidance?

For qualifying auto-accident or workplace-injury appointments, review the Medical Massage information and call the studio before booking.
