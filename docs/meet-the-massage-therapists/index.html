Medical and therapeutic massage

Lori Lynn Sykas, LMT

Lori’s published profile describes two decades of medical and therapeutic massage experience, with focused work around injury rehabilitation, orthopedic conditions, and tension patterns. Her approach is described as gentle, effective, compassionate, and holistic.

Pain-relief focused care

Emily Farmer, LMT

Emily’s published profile shares that she graduated from Stillwater’s Massage in Bothell and enjoys helping clients work toward pain relief. Her profile also reflects a hands-on, creative life rooted in family and the Marysville community.

Published license: 60716375

Founder and owner

Steffen Emard, LMT

Steffen founded Asgard Massage in 2014 after graduating from the Cortiva School of Massage in Seattle. His published massage style blends treatment work, deep tissue, and relaxation, guided by a desire to help people improve their lifestyle through regular therapeutic massage.

His philosophy is simple: care well for therapists so they can care well for clients.
