Wellness massage

A massage that starts with how you feel today.

Wellness massage at Asgard is not a one-size-fits-all experience. It is a conversation, a plan, and a session shaped around what you need in the moment.

Custom by design

Your goals guide the session.

The current Asgard site describes a customizable wellness massage that can draw from Swedish, deep tissue, Lomi Lomi, prenatal, sports, myofascial, and other appropriate approaches. The important part is not the label. It is having a therapist who listens and helps create a session that serves your goal.

Come in for quiet time, relief from an overfull week, more attention for a tight area, or a dependable self-care rhythm. Tell your therapist what would feel most helpful.
