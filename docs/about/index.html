About Asgard Massage

Local care, built around people.

Asgard Massage was founded with a belief that still guides the practice: when therapists are supported, they can bring their best care to the people who trust them.

The Asgard story

Founded to help more people feel better.

In his published profile, owner Steffen Emard explains that he first wanted to help people improve their lifestyle through regular therapeutic massage. That purpose grew into a clinic: a place where a team of like-minded therapists could extend that care to more people.

Asgard opened in January 2014 and serves Arlington, Smokey Point, Marysville, Stanwood, Camano Island, and nearby communities. The current site describes a mission to care for therapists, patients, and the community.
