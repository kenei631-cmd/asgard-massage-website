What should I tell my therapist?

Share what has been bothering you, what you hope to get from the session, relevant preferences, and any questions about pressure or technique.

Do I need to know a massage modality before booking?

No. Booking a massage is enough. The therapist can discuss your goals and help guide the session.

What if I need help with an injury-related appointment?

Call before booking. Qualifying auto-accident and workplace-injury appointments may have referral or claim requirements.
