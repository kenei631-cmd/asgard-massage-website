Massage modalities

Which type of massage should I book?+

Wellness Massage is the best place to start if you are unsure. Tell your therapist what is bothering you and what you hope to get from the session; they can customize the approach.

Can I book the same day?+

Same-day appointments may be available. Check online booking for live availability or call the clinic for assistance.

Do you work with auto or workplace injuries?+

Yes, Asgard provides medical massage for qualifying auto-accident and workplace-injury cases. Please call before booking so the team can explain the information required.

Do you offer gift certificates?+

Yes. A massage gift is an easy way to give someone time for themselves.
